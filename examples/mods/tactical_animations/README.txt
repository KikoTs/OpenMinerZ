Tactical Animations

Underhand fore-end grips, a side-presented pistol reload with a palm-under-base magazine push, fitted thumbs and a corrected knife hilt and thrust. Complete detachable parts and recorded mechanical foley. Original gameplay timing; looks and sounds only.

Scope: client
Version: 1.3.2

Enable in the main-menu Add-Ons screen before loading a world.
To edit, copy this folder into %LOCALAPPDATA%\OpenMinerZ\Mods first.
Validate with: openminerz_mod_sdk validate --strict <folder>
Test with: openminerz_mod_sdk run <folder> --seconds 10
Edit motion with rig-lab.bat. Retain AUDIO_CREDITS.txt and AUDIO_SOURCES.json.
