======================

The following components and media retain their separate licenses.

Bundled source:
  three.js 0.180.0 and controls: MIT, three.js authors.
  Full notice: tools/ModSdk/rig_lab/vendor/LICENSE-three.txt
  Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r180

Recorded audio:
  tools/ModSdk/recorded_foley contains CC0-1.0 recordings and Vinrax's
  CC-BY-3.0 cloth recording. Authors, source URLs, licenses and SHA-256 hashes
  are listed in sources.json; credits are in CREDITS.txt.
  Tactical Animations contains edited excerpts. Preserve its AUDIO_CREDITS.txt
  and AUDIO_SOURCES.json when redistributing that pack.

  Luau: MIT — https://github.com/luau-lang/luau

  zlib: zlib license — https://zlib.net
  bzip2: bzip2 license — https://sourceware.org/bzip2
  Brotli: MIT — https://github.com/google/brotli

The public runtime packager copies each installed dependency's complete
copyright/license notice from vcpkg into Notices/.

Original example art, models, fonts, synthesized example music and authored
animation tracks are generated by tools/ModSdk scripts. The explicit reviewed
media inventory is tools/release-assets.json. Recorded foley is the exception
described above. Native game geometry, textures, animation reference clips,
fonts and audio banks are imported locally and excluded from public packages.

Microsoft XNA/.NET/DirectX prerequisites and Valve's Steam runtime are not
included. Obtain them through your own game installation and official setup
tools; they retain their owners' terms.
