# --- Modern 1.9.9.8 (network version 4) -------------------------------------
# The native item, enemy and block tables hold ONE generation's numbers
# (Classic's). Every entry below is a value Modern changed.
4 ^enemy\.count$ Modern has 53 enemy types: HELL_LORD is inserted at 51 and ALIEN moves to 52. The wire id is translated (world::enemy_type_from_wire) but the Hell Lord has no native type and is shown as a Felguard.
4 ^enemy\.50\.(health|bullet_chance)$ Modern Felguard has 150 health and a certain bullet strike; the native table keeps Classic's 140 and 0.6, so this peer's copy dies 10 health early and reports the kill.
4 ^enemy\.51\. Index 51 is the Hell Lord in Modern (300 health boss Felguard) and the Alien natively; compared by index, so every field differs. See enemy.count.
4 ^item\.\d+\.gun\.recoil$ Modern retuned view recoil per weapon family. Recoil only moves the firing player's own camera and is not replicated.
