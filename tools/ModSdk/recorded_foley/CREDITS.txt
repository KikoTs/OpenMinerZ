# Recorded foley sources

Original downloads are identified by SHA-256 in `sources.json`. The pack ships
short mono excerpts, trimmed, faded, and gain-adjusted by `tactical_foley.py`.
Each generated cue's source, trim and gain limit is in the pack's `AUDIO_SOURCES.json`.

- **zer0_sol — Handgun Reload Sound Effect**, [source](https://opengameart.org/content/handgun-reload-sound-effect), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **SpringySpringo — Gun Reload Sounds**, [source](https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Recorded airsoft handling.
- **Brian MacIntosh (BMacZero) — Gun Reload Sound Effects**, [source](https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sound-effects), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **Brian MacIntosh (BMacZero) — Mechanical Sounds**, [source](https://opengameart.org/content/mechanical-sounds), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **Vinrax — Cloth swing sounds**, [source and creator](https://opengameart.org/content/cloth-swing-sounds), [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Recorded cloth, edited into quieter movement and pouch cues. Attribution must travel with these edits.

No endorsement by these creators is implied. The recordings retain their
respective licenses. Animation code and pack meshes have separate authorship.
